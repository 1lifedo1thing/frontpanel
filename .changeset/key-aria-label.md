A piano key can have a spoken name apart from its printed label. An option of `FpRadioGroup` or `FpCheckboxGroup` takes `ariaLabel`, which names the key for screen readers while its short `label` stays printed over it: `STD` printed, `Standard (EADGBE)` spoken. Without it, the printed label names the key, as before.
